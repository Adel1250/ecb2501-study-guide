The whole story in 4 lines

  1. Lecture 1: materials come in families (metals, ceramics, polymers, composites), and what's inside them decides how they behave.
  2. Lecture 2: atoms are a heavy nucleus plus light electrons, and atoms are "unhappy" until their outer shell is full.
  3. Lecture 3 + Section 2: to get full shells, atoms give, take or share electrons → bonds. Pull and push balance at a perfect distance.
  4. Lecture 4 + Sections 3 & 4: billions of bonded atoms stack into neat repeating patterns → crystals. We study one small "brick" of the pattern.
