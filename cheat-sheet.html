🧱 Lecture 1: Introduction

Big ideas

• Structure → Properties
• Science = why; Engineering = design
• Levels: subatomic → atomic → nano → micro → macro
• Choose by: conditions → deterioration → cost

Lists to know

• 6 properties: mechanical, electrical, thermal, magnetic, optical, deteriorative
• 4 families: metals, ceramics, polymers, composites
• Crystalline (metals) vs amorphous (glass)

⚛️ Lecture 2: Atomic structure

Definitions

• Z = number of protons
• A ≈ protons + neutrons
• Isotopes: same p, different n
• 1 amu = 1/12 of a ¹²C atom

Numbers

• NA = 6.022 × 10²³ per mole
• 1 amu/atom = 1 g/mol
• Shells hold 2, 8, 18, 32
• Full outer shell = stable (noble gases)

Forces & energy

FN = FA + FR
EN = EA + ER = −A/r + B/rⁿ
At r₀: FN = 0 = dEN/dr
r₀ = (A/nB)1/(1−n)
E₀ = −(A/r₀)(1 − 1/n)

What E₀ predicts

Larger E₀ → higher Tm
Larger E₀ → smaller αℓ
ΔL/L₀ = αℓ(T₂ − T₁)
Copper (1085 °C) ≈ 345 kJ/mol

Words

• Polycrystal: many grains + grain boundaries
• Anisotropic: depends on direction (single crystals)
• Isotropic: same in all directions (random grains)
